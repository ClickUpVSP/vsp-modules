Lesson 1

Welcome to the MSP Program

This is the start. Not another vague partner badge, but an actual path to packaging ClickUp into outcomes companies will pay for.

Why this matters

Businesses don't fail because they picked the wrong tool. They fail because a tool never fixes a broken process. That gap is the opportunity, and MSPs close it.

Every company on earth is trying to figure out AI right now. They buy platforms, spin up agents, watch demos, then stall. The gap between this is possible and this is working needs someone who can actually diagnose, implement, and own the outcome.

What is an MSP?

MSP stands for Managed Services Provider. You diagnose the pain, build and sell the system that solves it, implement that system, then grow a relationship on top of it. That stack is what makes you indispensable.

ClickUp is betting on MSPs because ClickUp cannot sit in every SMB office and turn intention into execution. You can.

Why now

There are 33 million SMBs in the US alone, and fewer than 1% of the service providers selling into them have real AI delivery capability. Demand is absurd, supply is tiny, and the early mover window is open right now.

You're creating a new professional category: the operator who packages AI into working business outcomes for teams that can't do it themselves.

The model

As an MSP, you resell ClickUp as part of a complete solution. You secure Enterprise Platinum at your partner rate, build it for the client, package it with your services, and deliver the whole thing end to end. The client gets admin access. You keep your IP, your service revenue, and a recurring share of the subscription economics.

What the program delivers

  • 20% recurring rev share on every workspace you resell and build, every year they renew
  • 100% of your services revenue on top
  • A solution that is yours: your builds, your IP, your portfolio
  • A defined service offer with real pricing and an SLA you control
  • A path to a scalable business that compounds

How do you get there?

Four modules, each stacking on the last. By the end of Module 4, you will have pitched, received feedback, passed certification, and gone live.
